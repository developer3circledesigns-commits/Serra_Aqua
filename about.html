About Us

About Serra Aqua Rivar Industries Pvt Ltd

Pure water backed by quality-focused processes, technology and quality control for homes, businesses and institutions.

Serra Aqua Rivar Industries Pvt Ltd is a packaged drinking water supplier producing 20 litre jars, and 2 litre, 1 litre, 500 ml and 300 ml bottles for homes, offices and institutions. Source water is treated through a chain of controlled stages, then balanced, stored and filled automatically to ensure every pack matches the same consistent standard.

Our Story

Pure Drinking Water Made With Care, Technology And Quality Control

Built with a focus on purity, hygiene and reliability, every step from source to sealed pack is managed in a controlled environment.

Our approach keeps the process systematic - sand filtration, activated carbon filtration, micron filtration, reverse osmosis, UV sterilization and ozonation are followed by mineral, TDS and pH balancing, hygienic storage, and automated bottling and sealing. This helps maintain consistent quality from one batch to the next.

With a strong focus on quality commitment and dependable delivery, we support homes, offices, institutions and businesses with scheduled and bulk supply tailored to their requirements.
